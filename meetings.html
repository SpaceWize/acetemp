Meetings

Two ways to sit down with Ace. Which one you book depends on how clearly you can already name the problem.

$500 / 90 minutes

Bring one decision. Leave with next steps.

Best for
You have already established your one problem with Ace’s Assistant and want to work through it.
What happens
  • Before: send a short overview of the issue, your role, and the decision ahead
  • Together: examine the situation, challenge assumptions, and compare possible next steps
  • After: receive a concise reviewed recap of options, open questions, and agreed actions
Request a Session

$200 / 60 minutes

Start the conversation.

Best for
You know something needs to change but not yet what the problem is — or you are interested in the Mastermind and want to learn more.
What happens
  • Once payment is received, Ace’s Assistant emails you questions to answer before you meet
  • Once answered, the Assistant requests your availability and schedules your Meeting
  • Meetings may continue longer than 60 minutes, subject to Ace’s discretion
Book This Meeting

Both meetings are held by phone, video, or in person by arrangement. Fees are in Canadian dollars.

The 90-minute session stands on its own. If further involvement would help, you can discuss Private Advisory or a separate on-site assignment. There is no requirement to continue.

If your 60-minute Meeting leads to Private Advisory or an Invested Aces Mastermind Membership within the following month, the $200 fee will be credited.
