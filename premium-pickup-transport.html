Premium Pickup & Transport

Ace offers Premium Pickup and Transport in his Cybertruck for situations where a standard rideshare feels too casual, unreliable, or lacking in presence. Ace arrives on time, with discretion, and in a vehicle and manner that matches the importance of the occasion.

This can be for airport transfers, client or investor meetings, events, time-sensitive deliveries, or any movement where the vehicle and driver need to feel intentional. When required, Ace will adjust his presentation to fit the setting so the arrival carries the right weight. (Formal, Business Casual, Street Nice, or Comfy Casual.)

There are two ways to access this:

  • People in the Invested Aces Mastermind can use it as a Membership benefit
  • Others can book it as a paid engagement

Paid Engagement Structure

Minimum

Rate
Local and Lower Mainland bookings are charged at $200 per hour with a firm 3-hour minimum of $600. Even if the actual time used is less than three hours, the full minimum applies.

Preferred

Rate
Preferred longer local engagements (4-6 hours) continue at $200/hr, or can be booked as a fixed 6-hour package of $1,100. Longer local days are quoted accordingly.

Extended Travel

Rate
Long-distance travel and overnight stays are available at a higher rate to properly cover time, vehicle, accommodations, and meals. These are quoted individually based on the itinerary.

Members of the Invested Aces Mastermind can access this service for $175/hr.

Availability Note

Ace hosts regular monthly Mastermind Meetings on the 2nd Tuesday and 3rd Saturday, plus Group Activities on the 2nd Saturday (Ace’s Running Group) and 3rd Wednesday (Aces Poker Night), and a Networking Event once every 3 months. Transport bookings must work around these commitments.

Bookings that support attendance at these events (pickup before, attendance, and return after) are welcome and can be arranged.

How to Book

Because availability is limited and bookings are handled personally, there is no online calendar.

To inquire or request a booking, use the Contact form and include your preferred date(s), approximate times, pickup/drop-off locations, and any relevant details about the occasion. Ace’s Assistant will respond to confirm availability and provide a clear quote.

A $200 deposit is required to secure the booking. The remaining balance is due upon pickup.

All rates are in CAD and exclude GST. Clear terms on timing, cancellation, and what is included are provided at booking.
