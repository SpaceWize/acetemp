Aces Running Group

A monthly group run with peers and professionals who value their time. The run takes place at a large local park and finishes with light refreshments and casual conversation.

When: 2nd Saturday of each month

Where: Williams Park, Langley Township

This is a straightforward social run. No pressure, just movement and good company.
