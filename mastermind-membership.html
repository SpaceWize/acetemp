Invested Aces Mastermind

The Invested Aces Mastermind is a private, application-only group for people who want consistent proximity to Ace and a vetted circle of others who take their own progress seriously. Members receive access to monthly Mastermind Meetings, Ace's Group Activities, and increasing levels of direct engagement with Ace depending on the tier they choose.

Membership runs for 12 months and is renewable. Membership fees can be paid monthly or annually. All tiers require an application and approval.

One-on-one Meetings and transport requests are scheduled through Ace’s Assistant.

* Meetings are typically 1 hour each, but can be longer at Ace’s discretion. Meetings for $2000 Members do not carry over if availability fills up.

**Premium Pickup & Transport requests must be for the Lower Mainland only, for a maximum of 3 hours, and single-day use. Additional time is billed at the $150/hour Member rate. Terms and conditions apply for additional distance, days or frequency.

Mastermind Meeting Details

  • Meetings are held on the 2nd Tuesday and 3rd Saturday of each month
  • Locations vary and are kept private. Exact details are shared by email two weeks in advance

Group Activities & RSVP

Members receive invites to all of Ace's Group Activities and must RSVP directly to Ace or his Assistant. Free Member tickets are issued only after an RSVP and are not visible to the public. Attendance without a confirmed RSVP is not permitted.

RSVP for Aces Poker Night is free for Members and non-members, with the understanding that an RSVP means an agreement to a minimum $100 buy-in. Members get first priority for RSVPs.

Full attendance and RSVP expectations are outlined in the Ace Community Code of Conduct.

Application Process

  1. 01

    Routine

    Fill out and submit a Membership Application and receive an email from Ace’s Assistant.

  2. 02

    60 Min Meeting

    Ace’s Assistant will schedule a meeting for you with Ace, so he can determine fit.

  3. 03

    Approval

    If approved, Ace’s Assistant will send you your Membership Agreement for signing.

  4. 04

    Payment

    Once your agreement is signed, you must pay your Membership fee to start enjoying your Member Benefits.

Approval typically takes 2-5 days, depending on Meeting scheduling.

All approved Members will sign a Membership agreement and be sent the Ace Community Code of Conduct to acknowledge before starting.

Who This Is For

People who value direct conversation, personal responsibility, and being around others who are actively investing in themselves. Location in or near the Lower Mainland of BC is strongly preferred due to the in-person nature of the Meetings and Group Activities.

Who This Is Not For

This is not for people looking for passive content, large unstructured networking events, or a place to promote their products and services. It is also not for those who are not prepared to show up consistently and contribute to the quality of the room. Additionally, people Ace will not consider VIP.
