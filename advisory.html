Ongoing Private Advisory

A private, one-on-one working relationship with Ace. This is designed for people who want consistent, continuous, and on-call access to Ace’s time, insight, and support.

The exact scope and access is built around what you need. It can include advisory, strategy, project oversight-style input, on-site visits, Premium Pickup & Transport, and other forms of direct support.

Don’t ask if this is personal or business. Ask whether you need an Ace Success Strategist, Evaluator & Transformationalist who works with you, regardless of your situation, so long as sustained mutual growth and respect are maintained.

At a Glance

Fee
Base package starts at $2,500 CAD / month for a 2 month minimum
Format
A minimum of three scheduled Private Meetings a month (in-person, online, phone).* Up to two on-the-spot calls as needed between them.**
Included
Benefits
Ongoing Private Advisory clients are welcome to attend Invested Aces Mastermind meetings and Ace’s Group Activities.
Add-Ons
Additional services, such as Premium Pickup & Transport, Public Speaking (teams/audience), and more can be included in your agreement.

The Approach

Fill out a contact form or email Ace’s Assistant at info@acespader.com.

Upon completing a short intake process and receiving approval, you can expect the following service and access.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    Ace listens and asks questions so you can talk through the situation until a clear mutual understanding is established. Some meetings are direct. Others are more casual and situational. Both work.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    Discussions are considered before Ace offers his thoughts on any next steps. He may suggest a new move, a small tweak to what you are already doing, or confirmation to stay the course.

  3. 03

    Implementation

    Between meetings or calls, the work is yours. You act on what was discussed, or you don’t. The success and implementation of the advice you receive requires honest and earnest adherence.

  4. 04

    Review and Refresh

    At the next meeting or call you start again at Inquiry: what you did, what changed, what didn’t, and why. Then the cycle repeats with better information than last time.

Ongoing Private Advisory starts at CAD $2,500 per month for the above mentioned format. Anything beyond the minimums (or a different mix of time and support) is discussed and quoted separately.

*Private Meetings run between 90-120 minutes and are scheduled through Ace’s Assistant. Allow one-two business days for scheduling.

**On-the-spot calls can run between 15-90 minutes and are taken by availability and agreement. If Ace is absolutely unavailable at the time of your call, he WILL get back to you at his earliest convenience, if possible.
